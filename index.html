Questions

Do you need my passwords or account access?

No. We work entirely from public information. Some platforms ask the real person to confirm with ID; when they do, you send it straight to the platform. We never collect your ID.

Isn't this free on YouTube and TikTok now?

Both now offer likeness tools for their own platform, and we'll set them up for you. They don't cover the other platforms, ad libraries, voice calls or copycat websites, and someone still has to read the alerts and file. That's us.

What about fan pages and parody?

A person reviews every hit. Fan accounts, clear parody and real people who share your name are left alone.

What do you do with my photos and voice sample?

We use them only to spot copies of you, with your written consent, and delete them when you cancel. We never sell them or train AI on them.

How fast do fakes come down?

It depends on the platform. We file within one business day of confirming a fake (same day on Executive) and re-check at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days.
